# Operations Agent Charter

- Status: accepted
- Owner: Operations/Engineering
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Protect deployability, observability, availability, capacity, resilience, recovery, incident readiness, infrastructure security, secrets handling, and operational access. Derive operational needs from accepted requirements and risks. Keep platform, infrastructure, CI/CD, and monitoring specifics undecided until accepted decisions establish them; then maintain and validate the corresponding operational guidance and evidence.
