## Purpose

Use chapter 1 of *Quasar Enterprise* as inspiration for requirements engineering and the business functionality of the tour operator business information system. The source informs discovery; it does not define project requirements by itself.

## Source range

- Analyze PDF pages 11-70 inclusive.
- These correspond to printed pages 3-62.
- Use PDF page numbers when operating on the file to avoid ambiguity.

## Included concerns

Focus on material that can inform:

- stakeholders and system actors;
- proto-persona inputs;
- business capabilities;
- actor goals and candidate use cases;
- operational workflows;
- business rules and information needs;
- domain terminology and conceptual models;
- candidate bounded contexts;
- quality concerns and constraints relevant to the business information system.

## Excluded concerns

Ignore management guidance, project-management concerns, organizational change advice, software-development methodology, and similar methodological discussion unless a passage directly establishes a material business-system requirement or constraint.

Do not extract candidates while preparing or updating this brief.

## Language

The source is German. All analysis, terminology proposals, models, requirements, diagrams, and other repository artifacts must be written in English. Translate concepts by meaning and domain usage rather than word-for-word. Retain a German term only when needed to resolve ambiguity, and pair it with the proposed English term.

## Analysis workflow

1. Extract PDF pages 11-70 while retaining headings, lists, tables, and diagram context where possible.
2. Assess whether the embedded text is reliable; use OCR only where extraction is inadequate.
3. Summarize the business-functional content and discard excluded methodological material.
4. Identify candidate stakeholders, actors, proto-persona inputs, capabilities, use cases, workflows, rules, information needs, terminology, domain boundaries, quality concerns, and constraints.
5. Consolidate duplicates and translate the results into consistent English project terminology.
6. Review candidates against the tour operator project scope before promoting them into authoritative project documents.

## Evidence policy

- Per-result labels describing whether an item is quoted, inferred, or assumed are not required.
- Page-level citations are not required in the resulting project artifacts.
- Do not present a book-derived candidate as an accepted project requirement without project-specific review.
- Personas remain proto-personas until supported by appropriate user research.
- Preserve enough working context during analysis to resolve misunderstandings, even though formal page-level traceability is not required.

## Expected intermediate output

Produce a consolidated, English-language discovery report for review before updating the authoritative stakeholder, persona, capability, use-case, domain, or quality-requirement catalogs.
