# Design System Requirements

- Status: draft
- Owner: Requirements
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-14

One shared design system shall serve Customer Interaction and Staff Interaction. It shall share semantic tokens, accessible primitives, component contracts, state meanings, responsive rules, and content conventions while providing two profiles:

- a comfortable, expressive customer profile suitable for travel discovery, composition, the persistent AI travel advisor, offers, orders, payment, documents, and assistance;
- a compact, data-dense staff profile suitable for resource lists, filters, selection, detail/edit work, validation, and business-significant actions.

The profiles are not separate design systems. A component varies by profile only where density, hierarchy, interaction context, or user evidence requires it. The system will build on the selected Mantine foundation under [DR-0010](../../governance/decisions/0010-adopt-python-centered-modular-technology-stack.md), but shall specify product semantics rather than duplicate Mantine's catalogue.

The concrete tokens, components, patterns, examples, and governance are not yet defined. Their creation shall follow the [Design System Workflow](../../governance/workflows/design-system.md) and consume the [navigation maps](navigation-maps/navigation-maps.md) and [wireframes](wireframes/wireframes.md). Comparative sources provide inspiration only and do not authorize copied branding, assets, content, claims, or unsupported interactions.
