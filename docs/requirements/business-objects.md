# Business Objects

- Status: draft
- Owner: Requirements
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Document behavior, invariants, events, and lifecycle—not merely data structures. Scope every model to a bounded context.

Progress from business meaning to implementation:

1. Requirements defines conceptual objects, relationships, ownership, terminology, behavior, and invariants.
2. Architecture defines logical attributes, identifiers, cardinalities, integrity, lifecycle, history, classification, and privacy needs.
3. Implementation defines physical schemas only after an accepted technology decision.

AI-generated models require domain review; plausible names do not establish business semantics.
