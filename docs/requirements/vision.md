# Product Vision and Scope

- Status: accepted
- Owner: Requirements
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

## Vision

Christopher Columbus Travel's claim is **“Explore the world, beyond boundaries.”**

Enable Christopher Columbus Travel to offer individual travel alongside package travel. Customers can use an AI-enabled travel advisor to explore, compose, and refine travel around their individual needs and interact seamlessly with the tour operator from the initial idea through the travel itself and thereafter.

The defining value is not AI-generated conversation by itself. It is the ability to turn a flexible conversation into coherent, available, and orderable travel while preserving the tour operator's accountability to the customer.

## Target users and outcomes

- Customers can express goals, preferences, constraints, and changes conversationally instead of assembling travel only through fixed catalog navigation.
- Travelers receive individual travel composed from suitable travel services, while package travel remains available when it better fits their needs.
- Travel advisors can participate in or continue the interaction without requiring the customer to repeat already confirmed information.
- The tour operator can carry the agreed composition into its established travel order, payment, document, and settlement activities.
- Customers and travelers can continue using the AI-enabled travel advisor for relevant assistance during travel and for post-travel interaction with the tour operator.

## Product principles

- **Individual and flexible:** the customer can iteratively add, remove, replace, or refine travel components before committing to a travel order.
- **Seamless interaction:** relevant confirmed context follows the customer between the AI-enabled travel advisor and human staff, subject to applicable consent and access rules.
- **Continuous assistance:** the AI-enabled travel advisor supports the customer before ordering, during travel, and after travel rather than ending at travel order creation.
- **Coherent and orderable:** recommendations must respect plausibility and availability rather than presenting an attractive but non-fulfillable itinerary as ready to order.
- **Accountable:** automation does not obscure which commitments the tour operator makes to the customer.
- **Complementary:** individual travel extends the tour operator's package-travel business; it does not replace package travel.

## Scope

- Offering both package travel and individual travel.
- Conversational discovery and refinement of customer needs.
- AI-assisted composition of individual travel from travel services.
- Plausibility and availability checks needed to progress toward an orderable composition.
- Continuity between automated assistance, human assistance, and travel order creation.
- AI-enabled assistance during travel and for post-travel interaction with the tour operator.
- Integration of individual travel into the wider tour operator cycle.

## Proposed success measures

Targets require stakeholder agreement. Measure whether:

- customers can reach an orderable individual-travel composition from a conversational starting point;
- customers can revise that composition without restarting the interaction;
- a human travel advisor can continue an AI-assisted interaction using the confirmed context;
- proposed travel passes the required plausibility and availability checks before commitment; and
- customers understand the resulting composition, price, next step, and accountable tour operator; and
- customers and travelers can obtain relevant assistance during and after travel without restarting their interaction with the tour operator.
