# Story

Use this template for one independently reviewable slice of a feature's use case, such as the happy path, one alternative or exception path, one boundary condition, or one cohesive rule set. A story is a planning item, not an authoritative requirement.

## Actor outcome

State the observable outcome for the actor and why this slice matters.

## Parent feature and authoritative links

Link exactly one feature parent, its use case, the selected flow or rule references, and affected requirement IDs. Classify unsupported statements as assumptions or proposals.

## Slice and exclusions

Identify the slice type and the exact behavior included. Explicitly exclude adjacent flows, rules, and technical work.

## Acceptance examples

Provide reviewable Given/When/Then examples using synthetic data. Cover the selected path and relevant boundaries without broadening the slice.

## Dependencies and lifecycle considerations

State dependencies and relevant security, privacy, accessibility, data, test, operations, and management implications. Link supporting tasks or bugs where needed.

## Open questions

List unresolved matters with an owner and the evidence or decision required to close each one.

## Completion

- [ ] The parent feature, use-case slice, and requirement links are current.
- [ ] Acceptance examples for this slice pass or have review evidence.
- [ ] Authoritative artifacts and affected stable IDs are updated or linked.
- [ ] Residual risks, skipped checks, and follow-up work are explicit.
