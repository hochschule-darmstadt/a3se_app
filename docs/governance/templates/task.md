# Task

Use this template for concrete lifecycle, governance, analysis, decision, implementation, test, or operational work. A task organizes work; its outputs become authoritative only through the applicable review and acceptance process.

## Intended outcome

Describe the concrete result or deliverable and why it is needed.

## Preconditions and dependencies

Link issues that block or are blocked by this task. State required evidence, accepted specifications, decisions, access, or other entry conditions.

## Scope

State the work that is included and explicitly excluded.

## Affected artifacts

Link relevant requirement IDs, specifications, decisions, risks, source evidence, code, tests, infrastructure, or operational guidance. Do not duplicate their authoritative content.

## Approach

Describe the planned work at the level needed for review. Separate established constraints from implementation proposals.

## Acceptance evidence

Describe the observable artifacts, checks, reviews, or decisions that will demonstrate completion.

## Lifecycle considerations

Summarize relevant requirements, architecture, implementation, test, security, privacy, accessibility, operations, data, and management implications. Use `Not applicable` with a reason where appropriate.

## Open questions

List unresolved matters with an owner and the evidence or decision required to close each one.

## Completion

- [ ] Preconditions and dependencies are satisfied or explicitly waived with rationale.
- [ ] Intended artifacts and affected stable IDs are updated or linked.
- [ ] Acceptance evidence and relevant lifecycle reviews are present.
- [ ] Residual risks, skipped checks, and follow-up work are explicit.
