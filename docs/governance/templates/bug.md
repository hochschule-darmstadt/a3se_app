# Bug

Use this template for observed behavior that contradicts an accepted requirement, specification, decision, or reasonable operational expectation.

## Observed behavior

Describe what happened, including relevant error output or other privacy-safe evidence.

## Expected behavior

Link the requirement, acceptance example, contract, decision, or operational expectation that defines the expected result. If none exists, state that the expected behavior is a proposal requiring clarification.

## Reproduction

Provide the smallest repeatable sequence, frequency, preconditions, and synthetic test data. Do not include secrets or personal data.

## Environment

Identify the relevant version, configuration, deployment context, browser, device, or dependency without exposing credentials.

## Impact and severity

Describe affected actors, business or operational impact, scope, workaround, and proposed severity with rationale.

## Lifecycle considerations

Identify likely affected requirements, architecture, implementation, tests, security/privacy controls, data, operations, and risks. Distinguish evidence from hypotheses.

## Regression evidence

Describe the test or other evidence that would demonstrate the defect is fixed and prevent recurrence.

## Completion

- [ ] Root cause or unresolved uncertainty is recorded.
- [ ] Authoritative specifications are reconciled where necessary.
- [ ] Regression evidence passes.
- [ ] Residual risks, skipped checks, and follow-up work are explicit.
