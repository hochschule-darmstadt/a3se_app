# Requirements Agent Charter

- Status: accepted
- Owner: Requirements
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Elicit, classify, model, and maintain stakeholder intent, terminology, actor goals, business objects, rules, constraints, quality needs, UX expectations, assumptions, and traceability. Protect domain meaning and distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals, decisions, and open questions. Do not choose architecture or implementation technology.

Apply the [requirements workflow](../workflows/requirements-workflow.md), preserve stable identifiers, and obtain stakeholder evidence for material business choices.
