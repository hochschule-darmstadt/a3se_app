# AI Assistance Governance

- Status: draft
- Owner: Engineering
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Evaluate AI assistance as an engineering method and, if included, AI behavior as a product capability.

## Review dimensions

- correctness, completeness, reproducibility, and calibration;
- security, privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and licensing;
- bias, accessibility, exclusion, and stakeholder impact;
- explainability, contestability, human oversight, and accountability;
- autonomy, tool permissions, irreversible actions, and agent security;
- cost, latency, reliability, provider dependence, and environmental impact;
- professional responsibility and appropriate disclosure.

## Milestone reflection

For material AI-assisted work, capture the intended benefit, what the AI contributed, how humans validated or corrected it, failure modes observed, residual limitations, and what should change in the workflow. Record this in the owning review, decision, risk, or engineering artifact; do not retain unnecessary raw conversations.
